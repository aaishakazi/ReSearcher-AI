# 🚀 ReSearcherAI

**An Intelligent, Bilingual Document Analysis & Generative Learning Platform**
> **Transform static documents into dynamic learning experiences in seconds.**

*A proud submission for the Google × GeeksforGeeks AI Challenge.*

---

## 🌟 Vision

Reading a document shouldn't be a passive experience.

**ReSearcherAI** is an AI-powered research and study assistant that transforms PDFs into interactive learning experiences using **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash**. Simply upload your document, and the application analyzes its content to generate visual summaries, study materials, presentations, and an intelligent chat interface with precise citations.

> **Stop passively reading. Start actively learning.**

---

# ✨ Features

### 🧠 Interactive Mind Maps
Automatically converts complex topics into dynamic, interactive mind maps powered by **vis-network**, making relationships between concepts easy to explore.

### ⚡ AI Flashcards
Generate smart flashcards from any chapter or document to improve retention through active recall.

### 📊 Instant Presentation Generator
Create presentation-ready slide decks directly from your uploaded PDF in seconds.

### 💬 AI Chat with Page Citations
Chat naturally with your document. Every response includes citations pointing to the exact page in the PDF, and clicking a citation instantly navigates to the relevant section with highlighted text.

### 🌍 Bilingual Support (English & Arabic)
Designed for multilingual learning with full English and Arabic support, including automatic Right-to-Left (RTL) layout handling.

### 🔒 Privacy First
Your documents remain private.

PDFs are processed locally in your browser and only the extracted content is sent directly to your own **Google Gemini API** endpoint. No documents are stored on any central server.
